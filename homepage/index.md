# Tamar Ervin

My name is Tamar (she/her) and I am a PhD student in the Department of Physics at UC Berkeley advised by Professor Stuart Bale. I am interested in understanding the fundamental physical processes at work in space plasmas.

I work at the 
[Space Sciences Lab](https://www.ssl.berkeley.edu/) alongside Dr. Trevor Bowen and Dr. Alfred Mallet 
studying kinetic plasma processes using data from Parker Solar Probe, Solar Orbiter, and other space missions. 
I also serve as the electric and magnetic field (FIELDS) instrument suite PI, under the supervision of Dr. John Bonnell, 
on the upcoming [OCHRE](https://physics.uiowa.edu/news/2024/09/nasa-funds-student-led-rocket-mission) 
Sounding Rocket mission scheduled for launch early 2027. I previously worked on the 
[FOXSI-4](https://foxsi.umn.edu/launches/foxsi-4) Sounding Rocket Campaign.

I serve as the lead of the Science Organizing Committee (SOC) for the [Parker Heliophysics Scholars](https://psp-gateway.jhuapl.edu/website/HeliophysicsScholars) series. 
We are a zoom only, bi-annual meetings series for early-career folks to share their recent work and have invited speakers discuss recent topics of scientific interest.
