# Tamar Ervin

My name is Tamar (she/her) and I am a PhD student in the Department of Physics at UC Berkeley. I am interested in understanding the fundamental physical processes at work in space plasmas.

I work with Professor Stuart D. Bale, Dr. Trevor A. Bowen, and Dr. Alfred Mallet at the 
[Space Sciences Lab](https://www.ssl.berkeley.edu/) studying solar wind source regions and heating mechanisms 
using data from Parker Solar Probe, Solar Orbiter, and other space missions. 
I am the electric and magnetic field (FIELDS) instrument suite PI, under the supervision of Dr. John Bonnell, 
on the upcoming [OCHRE](https://physics.uiowa.edu/news/2024/09/nasa-funds-student-led-rocket-mission) ionospheric 
Sounding Rocket mission scheduled for launch early 2027. I previously worked on the 
[FOXSI-4](https://foxsi.umn.edu/launches/foxsi-4) Sounding Rocket Campaign.
